that they are refused. It returned refusals and templates. That is the finding,
and it is not a failure of the round: four of the five lanes returned the only
honest answer the record permits — the thing asked for does not exist, and no

stopped on a method limitation and checked nothing.

accountable authority, with at most one validation outstanding), every Round 8
decision fails on the same ground: **accountable authority is absent in every
lane.** No appointing body, no declarant, no sign-off authority, no live
environment, no dataset. Draft records with named TBD fields are the advance,
exactly as in Round 7. Nothing in this round authorises entry, baselining,
calibration gating, or any use of the three visualiser sites.
