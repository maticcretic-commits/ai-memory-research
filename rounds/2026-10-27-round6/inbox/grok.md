## Reply verbatim

Appointment problem (named, not restated): Neither the ISA nor the IGR identifies any individual, any appointing body, or any budget authority. The development chain therefore retains de-facto control over who occupies the roles, who can be removed, and who pays for them. That is the appointment problem.

Who appoints, removes, and funds; independence safeguards

No established appointing body exists for either role. In the absence of a named external authority, any appointment, removal, or funding decision defaults to the same development chain that produces the systems under review. Independence safeguards that would make appointment and removal by that chain impossible have not been defined, let alone locked. Consequently:

Appointment remains possible by the development chain.
Removal remains possible by the development chain.
Funding remains controllable by the development chain.

Until an external body with independent budget authority is named and accepts the roles, the formal independence required for certification is not present.

One person holding both roles; ownership of DeepSeek third-party relabelling and audit trail

One person may not hold both the ISA and the IGR. The records each role certifies are distinct and sequential: the ISA certifies the safety disposition of a case; the IGR certifies the governance process that produced and reviewed that disposition. Combining the two in a single individual collapses the separation between the safety finding and the process audit of that finding. The same logic bars either role from also owning DeepSeek’s third-party relabelling and audit trail. That trail is itself an input to the records the ISA and IGR must certify; ownership of the trail would place the certifier in the position of validating its own work product. Separation is therefore required by the nature of the certified records, not by a general preference for division of labour.

S4 appeal mechanism

No appeal mechanism has been established. The following elements remain undefined and therefore unlocked:

Who may appeal an ISA disposition.
On what grounds an appeal may be lodged.
Within what time window the appeal must be filed.
What happens to the disputed case while the appeal is pending (stay, continued processing under provisional status, or other treatment).

Because none of these elements is fixed, an S4 appeal process cannot operate.

What remains unlocked if no appointing body will sign; certification power of an unappointed role

If no appointing body will sign, the following remain unlocked:

Identity of the appointing authority for ISA.
Identity of the appointing authority for IGR.
Removal authority for each role.
Budget authority and funding source for each role.
Independence safeguards that would prevent the development chain from controlling appointment or removal.
The entire S4 appeal mechanism.

An unappointed ISA or IGR cannot certify anything at all. Certification requires a validly appointed holder of the role. Without appointment by a body that itself possesses independent authority, any document signed by an individual claiming to act as ISA or IGR lacks institutional force.

Top 5 findings

No individual, appointing body, or budget authority has been named for either the ISA or the IGR.
Appointment, removal, and funding therefore remain under the control of the development chain.
No independence safeguards that would make development-chain control impossible have been defined.
Concurrent holding of both roles, or of either role together with ownership of DeepSeek’s third-party relabelling and audit trail, is incompatible with the sequential and distinct character of the records each role certifies.
The S4 appeal mechanism is entirely undefined; no appellant class, grounds, window, or interim treatment of disputed cases exists.

Top 3 open questions

Which external body, if any, is prepared to accept appointment, removal, and budget authority for the ISA and the IGR?
What concrete independence safeguards (statutory, contractual, or institutional) would render development-chain control over appointment and removal impossible?
Until those bodies and safeguards are locked, is any certification issued under the titles ISA or IGR to be treated as void, advisory only, or provisionally valid under some other rule?

Status and consequence

Unlocked. No appointing body, no named individuals, no budget authority, and no independence safeguards have been established. The consequence of leaving the matter unlocked is that neither the ISA nor the IGR possesses institutional authority to certify any disposition or process; any document purporting to be such a certification is without force, and the safety and governance records that depend on those certifications remain unauthenticated.
