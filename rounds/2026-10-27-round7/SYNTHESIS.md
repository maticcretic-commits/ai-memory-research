## Lane 3 — Gemini, frozen telemetry acceptance protocol: COMPLETE-WITH-TBD-FIELDS

Gemini produced a draft pre-registrable protocol, PROTOCOL-MM-TEL-R7-001,
for the bounded recalibration probe. It specifies the exact 3-way prompt
("Quick classification: Outdated/Superseded, Factually Wrong,
Bad Timing/Interruptive"), the injection moment (explicit undo, manual
override, or latency above 2.5 times the running baseline), skip behaviour
(auto-dismiss after 3 seconds; Esc, click-away, or continued interaction
counts as dismissed/skipped), and exposure control (the user sees output and
the toast only; no confidence scores, internal rankings, trigger conditions,
calibration status, or arm assignment). Observed events and inferred motives
are separated in storage: raw timestamps and keypresses in an immutable log,
attribution labels in a derived table linked to a raw event ID.

The four declared measures with pre-stated acceptance criteria are: primary
task slowdown at or below 15% against the passive baseline; intra-rater
kappa at or above 0.70 after ten interactions; task completion within plus or
minus 2% of the passive baseline; and cumulative prompt overhead below 1.5%
of session time. The random-prompt control is a constant 1% sample; if the
triggered-phase label distribution matches the control while completion stays
within 2% of baseline, the measurement is falsified as a prompt artefact.
The fail-closed falsifiers are the same three triggers: slowdown above 15%,
kappa below 0.70, or a triggered distribution statistically indistinguishable
from the control (chi-square p above 0.05 over 50 events). Start, stop, the
50-event cap, and the 3-prompts-per-15-minutes cap are kept exactly as in
settlement C.

For the two uncalibrated parameters, Gemini does not invent values. It
specifies a calibration procedure: passive telemetry from at least 1,000
continuous sessions across at least 50 distinct users, theta-variance set at
the 85th percentile of the passive variance distribution, the latency cap set
so total UI overhead stays below 1.5% of session time, and sign-off by the
Lead Telemetry & Evaluation Architect. If baselines cannot be collected,
active sampling cannot launch and the system defaults to 100% passive
telemetry, accepting the why-gap. Owner, theta-variance, latency cap, and
calibration sign-off authority all remain UNASSIGNED or UNCALIBRATED. The
cold-start question is the sharpest in this lane: with zero real production
decision pairs in the record, which live environments would supply the 1,000
passive sessions, and how would a micro-toast distinguish a memory failure
from network jitter that also trips the 2.5-times latency trigger?

This draft closes several of the gaps Perplexity named in its re-audit of
settlement C (exact wording, injection, skip, exposure, separation, measures,
control, falsifiers, calibration procedure). It does not close the authority
gap, and it cannot supply the baselines. Settlement C therefore remains
UNLOCKED/UNVERIFIED under Perplexity's rule, even though its protocol is now
the most specified record in the round.

## Lane 4 — DeepSeek, frozen calibration protocol: COMPLETE-WITH-TBD-FIELDS

DeepSeek produced a draft pre-registrable calibration protocol. The metric is
stated-confidence-only Expected Calibration Error with reliability-diagram
monotonicity as a co-required criterion; accuracy alone is inadmissible. The
pass rule is frozen in form, not in number: point estimate at or below the
threshold AND upper confidence bound at or below threshold plus 0.02, with
the 0.02 itself illustrative until the authority sets it. A confidence
interval straddling the threshold is inconclusive, not a pass. The numeric
threshold is TBD, its owner UNASSIGNED pending the calibration-gate
authority, and no number may be entered after scoring begins.

Sample discipline is explicit: ECE is unstable below roughly 100–150 pairs
(standard deviation about 0.038 at 50, 0.026 at 100, 0.015 at 250), so the
scored set must be at least 100–150 resolved pairs, exact N TBD. The 14-pair
probe is coverage and validity only and must never be calibration-gated;
the 80-pair run may carry only a provisional gate, sits below the stability
floor, and is diagnostic if no pre-registered number exists. Bootstrap
parameters (resample count, seed) are TBD at freeze. The adjudicator role is
specified (independent, not the model builder, not the threshold owner,
label fixed before confidence is revealed) but its holder is unassigned.
The freezer role is unresolved, with no appointing body named. The
staged-versus-real comparison is unresolved because zero real production
decision pairs exist. The mechanism-fault override stands regardless of a
passing ECE: uniform underconfidence across all bins, AUROC near 0.5, and the
stated discordance pattern route the defect as a mechanism fault and stop
re-scoring. Settlement D remains UNLOCKED/UNVERIFIED: metric and override
specified, number, authority, adjudicator, freezer, and real-pair source
all absent.
