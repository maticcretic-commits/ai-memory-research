   frames. This applies to Shorts and Reels alike.
2. **Answers.** The MCQ answer never appears in the video or the
   voiceover. The answer line goes at the end of the description only.

   Affairs episodes are one Hinglish (Roman-script) version only, with
   a licensed photo for every story and real licensed video B-roll
   attempted for all five stories, at least two actually carrying a
   clip. Hindi voice only, Roman-script text kept.
4. **Channel separation.** Daily Current Affairs posts only to its own
   channel (UCsOqDVFHEFv1f9rb4zF9nnA, handle @DailyCurrentAffairs-s1p,
   account maticcretic@gmail.com). Meme work posts only to Meme Adda
   (UCnQJQkNKfUv8GnE-Llkv-Jg, handle @memeaddaindia, account
   niteshlhsnda@gmail.com). Nothing crosses between channels. Channel
   ID is verified before every upload.
5. **Licensing.** Published assets use properly licensed equivalents;
   source and licence are recorded. Film and TV clips are never used.
6. **Exceptions.** An exception to any rule above is NOT PUBLISHED
